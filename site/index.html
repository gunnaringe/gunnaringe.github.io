About

I'm a technical leader and software engineer at Cisco, in the Provider Mobility group. I live in Melhus, just south of Trondheim, with my wife, our two daughters, and a dog.

I wrote a lot of code before AI came along. These days I review more of it than I write, but what I enjoy most hasn't changed: good architecture and robust systems that keep working.

Outside work, I'm on the board of Melhus skolekorps, our local marching band, and I help out at the local church, mostly behind the sound mixer. Whatever time is left, my 3D printer is usually busy, or I'm setting up Home Assistant automations around the house.
